My favorite sketch papers. Napkins, paper towels and junk mail envelopes. Also, if I remember correctly, before they were [@blackwing](https://www.instagram.com/blackwing/) pencils. They used to be called palomino. I think I still have a couple of boxes of these and are still one of my favorites to draw with. Truthfully, I was just about to toss these in the trash, that’s why they’re all crumpled up. But I did like them enough for a quick snapshot for posterity.
